About Me

Strategic by nature.
Visual by instinct.

I'm a Senior Designer & Strategist based in Somerville, MA with a background in visual design, UX, and brand execution across digital, print, and experience design. I've worked with Fortune 500 clients and global brands, producing work that's on-brand, accessible, and built to scale.

I've worked inside the brand systems of Fidelity, Red Hat, Akamai, Brown Brothers Harriman, and J&J, designing everything from full marketing sites and infographics to illustrated systems and campaign assets. At BBH, I designed cross-platform assets for 20+ global marketing campaigns and led WCAG-compliant accessibility implementation for international financial products.

My approach is rooted in human behavior and psychology. I don't just make things look good, I think hard about why people do what they do and design around that. That lens has driven outcomes like a 47% increase in learner engagement on a redesigned e-learning experience.

I thrive in rooms, in-person, collaborative, with a team that's actually trying to solve something hard together. I'm currently building toward more design leadership opportunities.
